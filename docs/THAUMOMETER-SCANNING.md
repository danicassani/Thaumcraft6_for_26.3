# Thaumometer scanning

The equipped meter is held in a permanent two-handed viewing pose, with the opposite item visually hidden. Walking uses the native view bob and respects its client option. Hold Use for 20 game ticks (one second at normal tick rate) to scan, adding a small tremor to the already raised lens. The native fingertips are anchored at the lower sides of the frame, with the forearms angled back toward the player; wide and slim skins use their respective palm offsets. Releasing Use or switching items early cancels the scan. The server chooses the target on completion, using the existing nine-block ray with block occlusion and loaded-chunk checks. Knowledge is never awarded from a client render or particle callback.

Sneaking while using the meter scans the aimed world target, including chests and other interactable blocks, without opening or activating them. Forge's `onItemUseFirst` claims the crouched action before the block can handle it; ordinary standing interactions retain their usual priority. The opposite-hand scanning mode has been removed. The opposite item remains visually hidden by the two-handed pose, but is never moved or consumed. The meter works in either hand. Items can still be dropped into the world to study them.

Scanning is explained in the First Steps entry of the Thaumonomicon, in the narrator's voice, without key names or exact timings. The passage is available before crafting the meter and remains readable after completing the entry. The HUD displays aura readings and a contextual target card with discovery status or known aspect icons; it carries no permanent control instructions. See THAUMOMETER-SIGHT.md for targeting, privacy and dropped-item verification.

Successful new observations play the vanilla experience-orb pickup sound. World scan attempts also restore the original scan sound and sixteen original TC6 rune glyphs, extracted into the native particle atlas. Repeated observations do not award knowledge or play the success sound again. Each attempt is gated by its own one-second use duration, without a subsequent item cooldown. This prevents vanilla from falling through to placing or using the opposite-hand item while Use remains held.

The archived original `ItemThaumometer` performs its scan immediately and its `drawFX` method emits runes; the held one-second animation requested here is implemented explicitly using modern item-use timing and Forge's first-person hand render event. No legacy OpenGL renderer is carried over.

`-PbookProbe=scan runServer` enables the opt-in native `ScanProbe` integration fixture. It checks duration, early release, sneaking world targets, both hand arrangements, retained items, five interactable block hooks, repeated observations and world scanning. `-PbookProbe=scan-client` enables a real-client scene for first-person animation and input verification. Normal release builds exclude these fixtures.

Port.78 adds `-PbookProbe=interactable-client`: native client input verifies chest, furnace, lever and offhand-meter scans without menus, lever activation or lost items.
